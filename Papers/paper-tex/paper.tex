\begin{abstract}
This paper presents an experimental investigation of optimal prompting strategies for gender neutral educational content. We systematically evaluate four prompting approaches—Raw, System Prompt, Few-Shot, and Few-Shot with Verification—across 25 educational paragraphs using OpenAI GPT-4.1-mini. Our controlled experiment comprising 300 individual trials demonstrates that Few-Shot prompting with verification and System prompt mechanisms achieves gender bias reduction while maintaining high text fluency and semantic fidelity. Through statistical analysis including ANOVA testing,BLEU-4 score, we give practical, research-based advice to help educators create learning materials that include all students. The study contributes both methodological frameworks for bias evaluation and practical guidelines for implementing gender-neutral content generation in educational technology systems.
\end{abstract}
