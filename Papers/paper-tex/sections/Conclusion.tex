This study demonstrates the significant potential of structured prompting strategies for mitigating gender bias in LLM-generated educational content. Through 300 controlled experiments, we established that Few-Shot prompting with verification mechanisms achieves 84.1\% bias reduction while maintaining semantic fidelity (BLEU-4: 0.77). Notably, System Prompt also demonstrated strong performance with comparable bias reduction rates, indicating that both verification-enhanced and direct instruction approaches can effectively address gender bias.

Our key findings include: verification mechanisms provide substantial improvement over basic prompting approaches, structured strategies maintain content quality while reducing bias, and consistent performance across experimental repetitions enables reliable implementation.

For educational technology systems, we recommend adopting either Few-Shot + Verification or System Prompt approaches, as both showed strong bias mitigation with minimal performance differences. These strategies should include concrete examples of gender-neutral transformations and clear instructions for inclusive language.
