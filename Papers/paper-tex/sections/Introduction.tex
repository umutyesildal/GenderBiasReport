Large Language Models (LLMs) in educational technology offer unprecedented content generation capabilities but inherit gender biases from training data, potentially perpetuating stereotypes in learning materials \cite{urchs2024chatgptbias, zhao2024multilangbias, lee2024lifecycle}. As educational institutions increasingly integrate AI-generated content \cite{beale2025revolution, pelaez2024impact}, ensuring gender neutrality becomes essential for inclusive education.

Recent research demonstrates that structured prompting strategies can effectively mitigate gender bias \cite{zeng2024debiasprompting, savoldi2024neutraltranslation}, yet existing studies focus on general text generation rather than educational content where pedagogical effectiveness must balance with inclusivity \cite{crawfurd2024sexist, nilsson2021chemistry}. This paper evaluates four prompting strategies for gender bias mitigation in educational content using OpenAI GPT-4.1-mini across 300 controlled experiments.

Our research addresses: \textit{Which prompting strategy most effectively reduces gender bias in LLM-generated educational content while maintaining text quality?} We provide evidence-based recommendations for educators implementing gender-neutral content generation systems\footnote{\url{https://github.com/umutyesildal/GenderBiasReport}}.
