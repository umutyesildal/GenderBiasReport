Recent advances in prompt engineering demonstrate significant potential for mitigating gender bias in LLM outputs. Zeng et al. (2024) found few-shot prompting with explicit examples achieves substantial bias reduction across multiple models \cite{zeng2024debiasprompting}. Savoldi et al. (2024) achieved 70\% gender-neutral translations in GPT-4 through systematic prompting \cite{savoldi2024neutraltranslation}. However, existing research lacks systematic comparison of prompting strategies specifically for educational content. Our study addresses this gap through comprehensive evaluation of four prompting approaches in educational contexts.
